Ending Your Engaged Learning Experience

End and Share your experience here

A project is not complete simply because the final deliverable has been handed over. A responsible ending helps the partner continue the work and helps students turn experience into lasting knowledge.

Close the Project Responsibly

Delivering useful work is only part of a successful project. Your partner should receive accessible files, clear documentation, and enough information to maintain or build on what your team created.

Prepare a Clear Handoff

  • Organize code, design files, research, and reports in accessible formats.
  • Write instructions explaining how to use, update, and maintain the deliverables.
  • Review the completed work against the original project scope.
  • Confirm that the partner has received all promised materials.

Close the Relationship

Thank the partner, communicate final responsibilities, and agree on any appropriate future contact or support.
